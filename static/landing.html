What we check.

The mistakes AI coding tools make most often. Up to 70 checks, mapped to the OWASP Top 10.

🔑 Leaked keys

API keys, tokens and passwords anyone can copy from your app or site.

🔒 Connections

Data sent without HTTPS, fake certificates accepted, missing security headers.

💉 Code that can be tricked

SQL, HTML and commands built from what users type.

👤 Logins and access

Weak login tokens, admin features unlocked in the browser, open CORS.

📂 Exposed files

.env files, backups, .git folders and source maps anyone can download.

📦 Risky libraries

Known security holes, and packages AI tools invented that attackers can register.

🕵️ Privacy

Trackers that start before consent, and the personal data your app can reach.

✍️ Settings

Debug builds, unsafe app settings and how your Android or iPhone app is signed.

Questions.

Do you keep my code?

No. Uploads and reports are deleted after 24 hours, and signing keys or .env files are never stored.

I don't have the source code. Can I still use it?

Yes. Send your Android app (.apk), your iPhone app (.ipa) or your website's address. Fixing needs the source code.

Which apps work?

Android and iPhone apps, websites, and projects in JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js, Python, Kotlin or Java, including apps built with Lovable, Bolt, Cursor, Replit or v0.

Is an AI deciding what's wrong?

No. Fixed security rules find and rate the problems; the AI explains them in plain words and writes fixes, which a second scan must prove.
